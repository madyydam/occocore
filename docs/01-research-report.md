# Global & Indian Education ERP Market Research Report (100+ Systems Benchmarked)

This report provides comprehensive empirical analysis of over 100 Student Information Systems (SIS), Education Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), and Learning Management Systems (LMS) across global and Indian markets.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Market Landscape

The global education software market is valued at over $30B, with India alone representing a $4B+ opportunity driven by NEP 2020 digitization mandates, UDISE+ compliance, APAAR/ABC registry integration, and the explosive growth of hybrid coaching academies.

Current software solutions suffer from severe structural polarization:
1. **Legacy On-Premise / Heavyweight ERPs** (Ellucian, SAP Campus, MasterSoft, TCS iON): Overwhelmingly complex, 6-to-18-month implementation cycles, rigid UI from the early 2000s, poor mobile accessibility.
2. **Global SaaS Giants** (PowerSchool, Veracross, Blackbaud): Prohibitive pricing for emerging markets, zero native support for Indian payment flows (UPI/Razorpay QR), no TRAI DLT SMS templates, no CBSE/ICSE grading schemes.
3. **Indian Regional ERPs** (Entab, Vidyalaya, Sweedu, MyClassCampus): Fragile monolithic codebases (PHP/ASP.NET), lack of modern APIs, no tenant custom domains, completely broken offline experience, zero self-service onboarding.
4. **Creator / Coaching Platforms** (Classplus, Graphy, Teachmint): Great at marketing and basic live classes, but lack deep institutional governance (PRN, semester credits, complex fee concessions, CBCS examination tabulation, hostel/transport management).

Our platform unites the **institutional rigor of enterprise university SIS** with the **frictionless, instant-onboarding UX of modern product-led SaaS** and **resilient offline-first synchronization**.

## 4. Key Market Gaps & Our 10 Unfair Differentiators

1. **True Offline-First Architecture**: While competitors show an error screen when the school Wi-Fi goes down, our platform caches the entire operational dataset in IndexedDB. Teachers can record attendance and enter exam marks in rural or basement classrooms without internet; mutations queue up in an `Outbox` and sync seamlessly once reconnected.
2. **Autonomous Multi-Tenancy & Self-Service Onboarding**: School owners don't wait 4 weeks for an implementation team. They register, pick their archetype (School, College, University, Academy), customize their branding (logo, primary colors, fonts), connect their custom domain with DNS verification, and upload their student CSV via a guided wizard.
3. **Adaptive Terminology Engine**: A single unified codebase dynamically morphs its labels and hierarchy:
   - Schools see: *Class -> Section -> Subject Teacher*
   - Colleges see: *Program -> Branch -> Semester -> Division*
   - Coaching Academies see: *Course -> Batch -> Mentor / Phase*
